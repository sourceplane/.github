## Sourceplane

**Platform discipline as code.** We build tools that let teams declare their platform's standards once — as intent — and have every repo, pipeline and coding agent follow them.

### What we build

- **[orun](https://github.com/sourceplane/orun)** — the open-source intent compiler. Declare your platform's standards as intent, package them as baselines, ground your coding agents in them, and let a deterministic runner plan and execute. One Go binary.
- **[Orunbase](https://orunbase.com)** — the agentic developer portal: product context, golden paths, a guardrails engine and living docs, on top of orun. Free for up to 3 engineers.
- **Baselines** — production-ready starter kits (Next.js, Astro, Turborepo, commerce, CMS and more) on Cloudflare Workers + Supabase, each shipped with Orun disciplines. Browse the pinned and listed repos below.

### Who it's for

SREs, platform engineers and startups taking an MVP to production — with the platform practices top engineering orgs use, on the infra you choose.
